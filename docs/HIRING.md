## How I communicate

**Engineering:** reproduction → affected boundary → suggested fix → regression test.

**Leadership:** exposure → business impact → options → decision owner → next action.

**Legal and privacy partners:** agreed requirement → technical control → evidence → limitations. Legal interpretation and regulatory applicability remain with the responsible specialists.

**Incidents:** confirmed facts → working hypotheses → containment decision → recovery criteria → lessons and follow-up controls.

## Evidence and limits

- Public repositories are lab, reference or design work; their maturity varies.
- Professional outcome metrics are labelled P2, owner-reported, not independently verified by the public projects.
- The ledger records missing comparison periods and public implementation gaps. A placeholder test is not proof of a detection gate.
- Veriseya has a public frontend and limited pilot; a full hosted API/worker is not yet live. The preview does not imply certification or complete legal/compliance coverage.
- I do not publish employer code, customer data or private audit evidence.
